# ADR 0008: MIT license

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-10-17
- Resolves: OPEN-QUESTIONS Q3

## Decision

Plan R is released under the MIT license (`LICENSE`), the same license the
Plan 9 Foundation uses for the Bell Labs release in `../plan9`.

## Consequences

- Plan 9 code or comments may be adapted when useful, as long as the MIT
  notice is kept.
- The Lucida fonts shipped with Plan 9 are under a separate license and
  are not used (PORTING-MAP.md).
